Privacy notice

The short version: no ads, no tracking, and nothing about you leaves your own device.

Last updated 20 October 2026

Stored on your device only

Your saved peaks, preferred pace, route figures, theme and a copy of the latest forecast for use with poor signal are kept in your browser. They never reach us, and clearing your browser data removes them.

No accounts, no cookies for tracking

There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics cookies. The site does not collect your name, email address or location.

Hosting and third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security (see GitHub’s privacy statement). Fonts are hosted with the site. The map library loads from cdnjs and map images from Thunderforest (or OpenStreetMap), so those services see a standard web request from your browser.
