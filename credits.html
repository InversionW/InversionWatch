Inversion Watch is built on other people's generous work. Here is everything the site uses, and the licence it comes under.

Weather data

Open-Meteo
Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed CC BY 4.0. Used for every forecast on the site. Open-Meteo combines models from national weather services and picks the best one for each location.
