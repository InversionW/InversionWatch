Inversion Watch gives estimates, not advice. Whether you go into the mountains, and how, is your decision and your responsibility.

What the site is

A free, personal project that estimates the chance of a cloud inversion on UK peaks, using public weather data. It is a planning tool for people who already know how to look after themselves in the mountains.

Forecasts are estimates

The chances, cloud heights, temperatures and start times shown here are calculated automatically and can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Weather in the mountains changes quickly and often differs from any forecast.

Not a safety forecast

Inversion Watch is not a substitute for official mountain forecasts. Always read the full MWIS and Met Office mountain forecasts before you set off, and check conditions again on the day.

Start times and routes

Suggested start times are rough estimates based on average walking speed and the route figures you enter. They do not account for your fitness, the ground, darkness, snow, ice or the weather. Nothing on this site is a recommendation that any route is suitable for you.

Your responsibility
